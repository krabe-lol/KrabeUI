Add `SignaturePad` (`krabeui/signature-pad`): draw a signature with mouse, finger or pen. `onChange` receives a PNG data URL after every stroke (always black ink on white, like paper) or `null` once cleared. Texts via `placeholder`, `clearLabel` and `label`; `aria-invalid` marks it as missing; `disabled` and `height` props.
