`Separator` (`krabeui/separator`) can show a label in the middle: children are rendered between two lines (`<Separator>or</Separator>`), horizontally or vertically. The lines stop at the label instead of running behind it, so it works on any background. A non-decorative separator takes the label as its accessible name. Without children `Separator` renders exactly as before. Also adds the Separator stories.
